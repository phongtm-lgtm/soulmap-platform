# SOULMAP Career Journey - Tu Vi Context

## Muc Tieu

Tai lieu nay mo rong cach dung Tu Vi cho Journey Su nghiep trong SOULMAP.

Career Journey khong nen chi dua tren `Menh - Quan Loc - Tai Bach`. Su nghiep thuc te con bi anh huong boi nguon luc gia dinh, anh em, moi quan he, doi tac, moi truong ben ngoai, suc khoe, tai san nen va dai van hien tai.

Nguyen tac noi dung cua SOULMAP van giu nguyen:

- Khong phan dinh menh.
- Khong noi chac chan tuong lai.
- Khong khuyen user dua ra quyet dinh lon theo kieu bat buoc.
- Dung Tu Vi nhu mot lop tham chieu de tao self-awareness va hanh dong kiem chung.

## Lop 1 - Cac Cung Bat Buoc

### 1. Menh

Dung de luan:

- Ban chat con nguoi.
- Khi chat lam viec.
- Cach user van hanh trong cong viec.
- Noi luc, tham vong, do ben.
- Cach phan ung voi ap luc.

Trong Career Journey, Menh tra loi cau hoi: `Toi la ai khi lam viec?`

### 2. Than

Dung de luan cach user thuc su song va hanh dong khi truong thanh.

Cac truong hop can bat:

- Than cu Quan Loc: su nghiep la truc doi manh.
- Than cu Tai Bach: dong luc tai chinh, kinh doanh, tien bac noi bat.
- Than cu Thien Di: su nghiep gan voi moi truong ben ngoai, di xa, thi truong, khach hang, quoc te.
- Than cu Phu The: su nghiep chiu anh huong boi hon nhan, partner, cong su than thiet.
- Than cu Phuc Duc: dinh huong nghe nghiep chiu anh huong boi nen tang gia dinh, phuc khi, gia tri tinh than.

Trong Career Journey, Than tra loi cau hoi: `Doi thuc te se nghieng ve huong nao?`

### 3. Quan Loc

Dung de luan:

- Nghe nghiep chinh.
- Chuyen mon.
- Dia vi, chuc danh, con duong thang tien.
- Kieu cong viec phu hop.
- Cach user xay dung su nghiep.

Trong Career Journey, Quan Loc la truc nghe nghiep cot loi.

### 4. Tai Bach

Dung de luan:

- Cach kiem tien.
- Nang luc quan ly tien.
- Mo hinh thu nhap phu hop.
- Lam cong an luong, kinh doanh, dau tu, commission, freelance.
- Moi quan he giua nang luc nghe nghiep va tien bac.

Trong Career Journey, Tai Bach tra loi cau hoi: `User nen tao gia tri va kiem tien theo cach nao?`

## Lop 2 - Cac Cung Rat Nen Co

### 5. Thien Di

Thien Di rat quan trong khi luan su nghiep hien dai.

Dung de luan:

- Moi truong ben ngoai.
- Di xa.
- Lam viec voi nguoi ngoai.
- Thi truong.
- Danh tieng xa hoi.
- Kha nang xuat ngoai, remote, cong ty nuoc ngoai.
- Su nghiep co phat trien khi roi khoi moi truong quen thuoc hay khong.

Case tot:

- Hop di xa, lam thi truong lon, lam voi khach hang ngoai nuoc, moi truong quoc te.
- Khong nen bo minh trong khong gian qua nho.

Case can than:

- Can chuan bi ky truoc khi doi moi truong, nhay viec, chuyen vung, di nuoc ngoai.

### 6. No Boc

No Boc lien quan truc tiep den career vi su nghiep hien dai phu thuoc nhieu vao nguoi khac.

Dung de luan:

- Dong nghiep.
- Nhan vien.
- Cap duoi.
- Cong su.
- Ban be xa hoi.
- Khach hang.
- Nguoi ho tro ngoai gia dinh.

Case tot:

- De gap quy nhan.
- Co team tot.
- Hop lam viec nhom.
- Co kha nang xay cong dong, network.
- Lam quan ly, founder, sales, consulting co loi.

Case can than:

- De bi dong nghiep pha.
- Chon sai cong su.
- Kho giu nguoi.
- Bi loi dung boi ban be hoac nhan su.
- Can can trong khi partnership.

### 7. Phuc Duc

Phuc Duc nen duoc dua vao career context vi su nghiep dai han khong chi la nang luc, ma con la suc ben tinh than va nen tang sau lung.

Dung de luan:

- Nen phuc gia dinh.
- Su nang do vo hinh.
- Tam the song.
- Do ben tinh than.
- Kha nang vuot khung hoang.
- Gia tri sau ben trong.
- Nghe co tao y nghia hay khong.

Case tot:

- Co hau thuan tinh than.
- De gap may hoac gap nguoi giup khi kho.
- Hop di duong dai, xay su nghiep ben.

Case yeu:

- Can cham suc suc khoe tinh than.
- Khong nen chon nghe qua bao mon.
- Career plan nen co nhip nghi va he ho tro.

### 8. Tat Ach

Tat Ach can xem vi su nghiep khong tach khoi suc khoe va nang luong.

Dung de luan:

- Kha nang chiu ap luc.
- Nguy co burnout.
- Diem yeu suc ben anh huong cong viec.
- Nghe nao nen tranh vi qua hao ton.
- Cuong do lam viec phu hop.

Luu y noi dung:

- Khong chan doan benh.
- Chi nen noi ve nang luong, ap luc, nhip lam viec, kha nang phuc hoi.

Vi du tot:

`Du lieu goi y ban nen tranh moi truong qua doc hai hoac deadline cuc doan trong thoi gian dai. Mot nhip lam viec ben vung se giup ban phat huy tot hon.`

## Lop 3 - Cac Cung Theo Truong Hop

### 9. Huynh De

Huynh De co the anh huong su nghiep qua anh chi em, ban be than, nguoi cung the he va warm network.

Dung de luan:

- Anh chi em ho tro von.
- Anh chi em gioi thieu co hoi.
- Gia dinh co network giup keo su nghiep len.
- Lam an chung voi anh em.
- Nguoi than cung the he nang do.
- Canh tranh, ganh trach nhiem, hao tai vi anh em.

Case Huynh De tot:

- User co the phat trien qua network gia dinh, anh chi em, ban be than thiet.
- Nen tan dung referral, partnership, family business, warm network.

Case Huynh De xau:

- Can ro rang tien bac, vai tro, trach nhiem khi lam viec voi nguoi than.
- Khong nen nhap nhang cong viec va tinh cam gia dinh.

### 10. Phu Mau

Phu Mau anh huong su nghiep qua nen tang gia dinh va cha me.

Dung de luan:

- Cha me nang do su nghiep.
- Gia dinh co truyen thong nghe nghiep.
- Duoc ho tro hoc hanh, von, quan he.
- Bi ky vong gia dinh dinh huong nghe.
- Phai ganh trach nhiem gia dinh nen kho tu do chon nghe.
- Xung dot voi cha me tao ap luc nghe nghiep.

Trong AI Career Journey nen phan biet ro:

- Nguon luc gia dinh.
- Ky vong gia dinh.
- Ap luc gia dinh.

### 11. Phu The

Phu The khong chi dung cho tinh yeu. Trong su nghiep dai han, vo chong hoac partner than thiet co anh huong lon.

Dung de luan:

- Hon nhan ho tro su nghiep.
- Vo chong la cong su kinh doanh.
- Partner dem lai network, von, co hoi.
- Hon nhan khien doi nghe, doi noi song.
- Xung dot tinh cam lam career cham lai.
- Can chon ban doi hoac cong su hop dinh huong.

Trong Career Journey, Phu The tra loi cau hoi: `Partner than thiet co tac dong the nao den quyet dinh nghe nghiep, tai chinh va huong phat trien?`

### 12. Dien Trach

Dien Trach khong chi la nha dat. Voi su nghiep, cung nay lien quan den tai san nen va kha nang tich luy dai han.

Dung de luan:

- Tai san nen.
- Bat dong san.
- Gia san.
- Van phong, co so kinh doanh.
- Kha nang tich luy tai san dai han.
- Nghe lien quan den dat dai, kien truc, noi that, hospitality, farming, real estate.

Case Dien tot:

- Co nen tang on dinh.
- De tich san.
- Hop xay business lau dai.

Case Dien manh:

- Co the hop bat dong san, quan ly tai san, khong gian, van hanh co so.

Case Dien xau:

- Su nghiep de bi anh huong boi chuyen nha cua, cho o, tai san gia dinh.

### 13. Tu Tuc

Tu Tuc co the lien quan su nghiep neu dien giai rong hon nghia con cai.

Dung de luan:

- Con cai.
- San pham tao ra.
- Doi ngu ke thua.
- Creative output.
- Du an ca nhan.
- Kha nang mentor, dao tao, xay the he sau.

Dien giai hien dai:

`Tu Tuc khong chi la con cai, ma con co the xem nhu san pham, du an, creative output hoac thu minh sinh ra cho doi.`

Case tot:

- Hop lam san pham.
- Hop sang tao.
- Hop giao duc, mentoring.
- Co duyen xay project rieng.
- Career phat trien khi tao ra thu co tinh ke thua.

## Cac Truong Hop Dac Biet Can Bat

### 1. Menh Tot Nhung Quan Yeu

- User co to chat nhung khong chac hop career path truyen thong.
- Can moi truong dung, mentor tot, hoac tu tao loi di.
- Khong nen chi nhin chuc danh.

### 2. Quan Tot Nhung Menh Yeu

- Co co hoi nghe nghiep, nhung ban than de ap luc hoac thieu tu tin.
- Can xay noi luc, ky nang, ky luat.
- Career plan nen co buoc nho, tranh nhay qua manh.

### 3. Tai Tot Nhung Quan Khong Noi

- Co duyen kiem tien hon la chuc danh.
- Co the hop sales, kinh doanh, freelance, dau tu, thuong mai.
- Khong nhat thiet phai leo corporate ladder.

### 4. Quan Manh Nhung Tai Yeu

- Co danh, co chuyen mon, co vi tri nhung tien chua tuong xung.
- Can hoc pricing, negotiation, monetization.
- Hop xay thuong hieu ca nhan hoac toi uu mo hinh thu nhap.

### 5. Thien Di Manh Hon Menh Hoac Quan

- Ra ngoai moi phat.
- Hop di xa, lam thi truong lon, cong ty nuoc ngoai, khach hang quoc te.
- Khong nen chi quanh quan moi truong quen thuoc.

### 6. No Boc Manh

- Su nghiep di len nho nguoi, team, network.
- Hop founder, manager, community, sales, partnership.
- Career action nen la mo rong quan he, khong chi hoc ky nang mot minh.

### 7. Huynh De Manh

- Anh em, ban be than, nguoi cung the he ho tro nhieu.
- Co the lam family business, referral, partnership.
- Can kiem tra sao xau de tranh nhap nhang tien bac.

### 8. Phu Mau Manh

- Gia dinh la nguon luc lon.
- Hop ke thua, tan dung nen tang, hoc tu truyen thong gia dinh.
- Neu co ap luc, AI nen phan biet nguon luc gia dinh va ky vong gia dinh.

### 9. Phuc Duc Manh

- Di duong dai tot.
- De co quy nhan hoac hau thuan tinh than.
- Hop nghe co chieu sau, gia tri, anh huong tich cuc.

### 10. Tat Ach Xau

- Khong nen goi y nghe chi vi hop nang luc.
- Phai xet suc ben.
- Career Journey can them risk ve burnout, stress, qua tai.

### 11. Phu The Anh Huong Manh

- Su nghiep co the doi huong sau khi co partner.
- Hop lam cung nguoi phoi ngau hoac duoc partner ho tro.
- Neu xau thi can tach bach tinh cam va business.

### 12. Dien Trach Manh

- Hop tich san, bat dong san, van hanh co so, kinh doanh co tai san co dinh.
- Career path nen tinh den tai san dai han, khong chi luong.

## Ket Luan

Career Journey nen chia du lieu Tu Vi thanh 3 lop:

```text
Lop 1 - Bat buoc:
Menh, Than, Quan Loc, Tai Bach

Lop 2 - Rat nen co:
Thien Di, No Boc, Phuc Duc, Tat Ach

Lop 3 - Theo truong hop:
Huynh De, Phu Mau, Phu The, Dien Trach, Tu Tuc
```

Huong dung cho SOULMAP khong phai de AI phan nghe, ma de AI tra loi:

- Cung nao dang tao luc day su nghiep?
- Cung nao tao luc can?
- Nguon luc den tu dau?
- Rui ro nam o dau?
- User nen kiem chung bang hanh dong gi?

Moi goi y nghe nghiep nen di kem mot hanh dong kiem chung cu the.
